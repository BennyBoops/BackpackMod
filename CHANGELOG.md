# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Fixed items being deleted or duplicated when the backpack was moved to a different inventory slot while its screen was open.
  - Backpack contents are now saved the moment they change instead of only when the screen closes.
  - The open backpack is locked in place while its screen is open, so it can't be picked up, dropped, shift-clicked or swapped with the number keys or the offhand key.
  - The screen now closes on its own if the backpack is no longer in the slot it was opened from.
- Added Forge and NeoForge support.
- Added Minecraft 1.20.1 support (Fabric, Forge and NeoForge).
- Fixed the homepage and source links in the mod info.

## 1.0.1

- Properly displays name after renaming in an anvil.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
